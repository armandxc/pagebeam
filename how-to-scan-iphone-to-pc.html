How to scan from your iPhone to a Windows PC, without a scanner

No scanner at home or in the office? Your iPhone can be one. PageBeam makes the iPhone show up on your computer as a network scanner, so the scanning apps already on your PC can scan from it. You do not need to install anything on the computer, sign in, or upload anything to the cloud.

What you need

  • An iPhone with iOS 17 or later and the PageBeam app.
  • A Windows 11 PC or a Mac on the same Wi-Fi network as the iPhone.

On Windows 11

  1. Connect the iPhone and the PC to the same Wi-Fi. On the PC, set that network to Private: Settings → Network & internet → Wi-Fi → your network.
  2. Open PageBeam on the iPhone and keep it open.
  3. On the PC, go to Settings → Bluetooth & devices → Printers & scanners → Add device and add “PageBeam iPhone”. You only do this once.
  4. Open Windows Scan (or another app that scans from a WIA scanner, such as NAPS2 or Paint) and click Scan.
  5. Approve the request on the iPhone, photograph the page, and it arrives on the PC.

Several pages? Prepare them on the phone first, then choose Feeder as the source in the scanning app.

On a Mac

Open PageBeam on the iPhone, then in Preview choose File > Import from Scanner and pick the iPhone. Approve each request on the phone.

Why not just email the photo to yourself?

Because the page lands directly in the app you are working in, with the scanner settings you chose, and nothing passes through email or the cloud. Each scan needs your tap on the phone, so nothing is sent without you.

Good to know

  • Keep PageBeam open on screen while scanning; iOS pauses it in the background.
  • Transfers stay on your local Wi-Fi but are not encrypted, so use a network you trust for private documents.
  • Apps that only scan through TWAIN are not supported.
