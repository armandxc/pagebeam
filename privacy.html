PageBeam privacy policy

Effective October 10, 2026.

PageBeam turns your iPhone into a scanner that computers on your own Wi-Fi network can use. This policy explains what the app does with information. The short version: we do not collect anything. The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no server of ours.

What the app handles, and where it goes

Information What happens to it
Pages you photograph Kept only in the app’s memory on your iPhone until you send them, discard them, or the app has been in the background for about 10 minutes. They are sent only to the computer whose scan request you approve on the phone. They are never uploaded to us or anyone else, and the app does not save them to your photo library or files.
Scan requests from computers A computer on your network asks the iPhone for a scan. The app shows the request and does nothing until you approve it. The computer’s network address is used only to deliver that scan.
Scanner name and a random device identifier Stored on your iPhone so computers recognize it as the same scanner. They are announced on your local network while the scanner is on, the way any network scanner announces itself.
Diagnostic report Created only when you tap Copy Diagnostic Report. It lists request types and timings, and leaves out network addresses, names and images. It stays on your clipboard until you choose to paste it somewhere, for example an email to support.
Purchases If you buy the unlock, Apple processes the payment. We receive no payment details or personal information, only Apple’s standard sales reports.

Permissions

  • Camera: to photograph pages.
  • Local Network: so computers on your Wi-Fi can find the iPhone and receive the pages you approve.

The app does not ask for your location, contacts, microphone, or photo library.

Security

Pages travel directly from your iPhone to your computer over your local network and never pass through the internet or our servers. Transfers on your local network are not encrypted, so use the app on networks you trust, such as your home Wi-Fi, and not on public or shared Wi-Fi when scanning sensitive documents. Every scan needs your approval on the phone, and you should only approve a scan you just started yourself.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.

Changes
