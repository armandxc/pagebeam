PageBeam support

Scan from your computer, using your iPhone

PageBeam makes your iPhone show up as a scanner on your Windows PC or Mac. Your computer’s own scanning apps use it like any network scanner. There is nothing to install on the computer, no account and no cloud.

Heads-up about public Wi-Fi. Transfers are not encrypted yet. On public Wi-Fi, like a café, hotel or school, other people on the same network could see the pages you send. That’s fine for a menu or a flyer; keep private documents to a network you trust, such as your home Wi-Fi. School and hotel networks also often keep devices apart, so the scanner may not show up there at all.

Set up (once)

  1. Put the iPhone and the PC on the same Wi-Fi network.
  2. On the PC, make sure that network is Private: Settings → Network & internet → Wi-Fi → your network → Private network.
  3. Open PageBeam on the iPhone and leave it open.
  4. On the PC: Settings → Bluetooth & devices → Printers & scanners → Add device, then add “PageBeam iPhone”. It can take up to a minute to appear.

Scan

  1. Open a scanning app on the PC, such as Windows Scan, and click Scan.
  2. The iPhone shows “Your computer wants to scan”. Tap Scan and send and photograph the page, or send pages you prepared earlier.
  3. The page arrives in the app on your PC.

For several pages, prepare them in the tray, then choose Feeder as the source in your computer’s scanning app. They arrive in tray order. Whether they’re saved as one document or separate files depends on the app: NAPS2 saves them as one PDF.

Works with

Tested on Windows 11: - Windows Scan - Paint (File → From scanner or camera) - NAPS2, using its WIA driver - PDF-XChange Editor (choose the scanner listed as WIA)

Other Windows apps that can scan from a WIA scanner should work the same way. Not supported: apps that can only scan through TWAIN.

On a Mac

  1. Put the Mac and iPhone on the same Wi-Fi and open PageBeam on the iPhone.
  2. In Preview, choose File > Import from Scanner and pick the iPhone. If the menu item is greyed out, bring PageBeam back to the front on the iPhone.
  3. Preview first asks for a quick low-resolution overview. Approve it on the phone, then click Scan in Preview and approve again.

If Preview stops responding, quit and reopen it.

Requires an iPhone with iOS 17 or later.

Scanner not appearing?

  • Same network. Guest networks, VPNs and some mesh or office networks keep devices apart.
  • Local Network access on the iPhone. Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network → PageBeam must be on.
  • Keep the app open. The scanner switches off when you leave the app or lock the phone, and comes back when you return.
  • PC network set to Private. Windows hides network scanners on Public networks.
  • Added before but now offline? Remove the scanner on the PC and add it again. Do this also after renaming the scanner.

Common questions

Does it work in the background? No. iOS only lets the scanner run while the app is open on screen. Keep it open while you scan.

Is it secure? Pages go straight from your iPhone to your computer over your Wi-Fi and never touch the internet. Every scan needs your tap on the phone. Transfers are not encrypted yet, so on public Wi-Fi other people on the network could see your pages. Use a network you trust for private documents.

Where are my scans saved? In your computer’s scanning app, wherever it saves them. The iPhone does not keep a copy.

Does it work with TWAIN software? No. It works with apps that scan from WIA scanners, such as the ones above. Many apps that offer TWAIN also let you pick WIA instead, usually under a driver or source setting.

Windows Scan said “Something went wrong”? Make sure PageBeam is open on the iPhone and click Scan again. Approve the request on the phone within about a minute.

Restore my purchase? Open PageBeam → About → Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.
